Your path · Module 5

Program your understanding

Learn anything by building a tiny program about it.

When a concept won't click, write a program about it. Turn a formula into a function. Draw what it does. Change the inputs and watch.

I made a graphing calculator in QB64 after meeting algebra and Desmos at 12. Building it taught me both the math and the code.

Try this: take one idea from school, define it as a function, and plot it in p5.js.
