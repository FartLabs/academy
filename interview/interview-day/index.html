Interview prep · Part 4

Interview day

Before, during, and after.

Before

  • Read about the company and the role. Know what they build.
  • Prepare three or four questions for them (below).
  • Virtual? Test your camera, mic, internet, and the coding tool the day before.
  • Sleep, eat, and arrive a few minutes early.

During

  • Think out loud. The interviewer is grading your thinking as much as your answer.
  • Ask clarifying questions before you code.
  • Stuck? Go big, then break it down. Say what you do know, solve a smaller case, and ask for a hint if you need one. Taking a hint well is a good sign.
  • Test your code before saying you're done.
  • Stay kind and curious, even when it's hard.

Questions to ask them

  • What does a good first six months look like in this role?
  • How does the team review code and share knowledge?
  • What's the hardest problem the team is working on?
  • What do you wish you'd known when you started here?

After

  • Send a short thank-you within a day.
  • Write down the questions you were asked while you remember them.
  • If you get an offer, you don't have to answer on the spot. Thank them and ask for time.
  • Rejection is normal, even for people who get great jobs. Learn something, then try again.
